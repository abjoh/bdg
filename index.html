A WISH FOR YOUR HAPPIEST YEAR

FROM MY HEART TO YOURS

No matter where life places us,

you are with me.

Even with all these miles between us, you are never far from my heart. You are part of me—in the quiet moments, in the hopes I carry, and in every little thing that reminds me of you.

You are my love, my heart, and one of the most precious parts of my life. For your birthday, I wish I could place the whole world at your feet. For now, I’m sending the truest things I have: my love, my prayers, and all my warmest wishes.

I know our road may ask patience of us, and that the people we love may need time to understand. Still, I hold on to hope. In sha Allah, one day we’ll look back on these miles and feel grateful we kept choosing each other—with kindness, courage, and faith. Until then, wherever you are, remember: you have a home in my heart.

Always yours, with all my love ♡

DISTANCE CANNOT DIM WHAT IS TRUE

FOR THE MILES BETWEEN US

A little promise, written in starlight

When the road
is long.

Two years of evenings, two skies, one moon—
your voice finding mine through the hush of the room.
The miles may lie wide as the darkening sea,
but none of that distance can loosen you from me.

We carry our hope with a patient, kind flame,
through questions, through waiting, still saying your name.
And if the road bends, if the answer takes time,
I’ll meet every hard day with your hand in mine.

For love is not hurried, nor proved by a day;
it grows in the choosing, the gentle, the stay.
In sha Allah, one day, beneath kinder skies,
we’ll trade all these goodbyes for hello in your eyes.

Till then, wherever you are, near or far—
you are my home, my heart, my brightest star.
